# Embryo Circuit Lab

Embryo Circuit Lab is Embryo-Zero's fork of [Logisim-evolution](https://github.com/logisim-evolution/logisim-evolution) (v5.0.0, commit 7c3343e). It teaches logic circuits with drag-and-drop simulation (Embryo Academy).
It is one of the Embryo chip tools, with Embryo Router (our Freerouting fork) and Embryo PCB (our KiCad fork).

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE.md) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
